The first release of Brain Dump: hold a button, ramble for up to 30 seconds,
and get back tasks, reminders, notes and ideas, each with a one-tap action.

**Which file?** Most phones: `BrainDump-1.0.0-arm64.apk`. Phones from before
about 2017: the `armv7` one. Not sure: `universal` works everywhere, it's just
bigger.

- **Hold to ramble.** Your words appear as you talk, with a live waveform and a
  30-second ring.
- **Sorted for you.** Tasks, reminders, notes and ideas, with dates like
  "tomorrow at five thirty" or "Thursday" worked out.
- **One tap to act.** Add to calendar, set a reminder, copy, share, tick off.
- **Works with no keys and no internet**, using your phone's speech recognition
  and built-in sorting rules. Add an OpenAI key for Whisper, and a Claude or
  OpenAI key for smarter sorting.
- **Type it** when you can't talk out loud.
- Past dumps, light and dark mode, and everything stays on your phone.
